# Draft update template

Use after every tool call that makes or changes a page or video. The person may not see the pictures the tool attached (some clients hide them), so the words and the link must stand on their own. Drop any line with nothing in it.

```
**{Made | Updated} {the page | the video} "{name}"** ({draft: campaigns cannot use it yet | finished}).
{One line on what it is, or what changed since the last version.}

{Page: the desktop picture, then the phone picture. Video: one still per scene, labelled Scene 1, Scene 2, ...}
Preview: {preview link} (for a sample: {sample values}; it records no views)
{Video: Narration about {n} seconds in {voice}. Listen: {narration link}. The stills show where each scene starts; motion and voice together need a test render.}

Still needed: {what you left out or guessed: a booking link, a picture they mentioned, a field their leads must have}

Next, if you like:
1. {a specific change that would improve it}
2. {another}
3. {Page: make the real page for one person (costs credits) | Video: watch a full test render (costs credits)}
```
